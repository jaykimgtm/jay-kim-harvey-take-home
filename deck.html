Part 1 · Expectation setting

What we decide in the first meeting

Language

Native Japanese is a given. Business English cuts the pool by more than half.

One English bar per role, and how we test it.

Calendar

Bonus days (Dec, Jun–Jul), fiscal year-ends and stock vests decide when people can resign.

Map each finalist's dates; price make-whole against them.

Pay

Sellers compare offers on base salary (固定給), so a US 50/50 plan looks thin.

A Japan band before the first offer talk; make-whole with Total Rewards.

Process

Long loops and counteroffers lose people. The casual meeting (カジュアル面談) should sell.

Four steps at most, and one person signs off.

First hire

Candidates ask: is Harvey staying in Japan, and who backs me here?

One story for all: MHM, NO&T, Mitsubishi Corp., and you.

Setup

Japanese contract, payroll and social insurance from day one? Candidates check before they resign.

People Ops confirms the setup before the first offer.
